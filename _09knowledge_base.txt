

Company: SmartShop

FAQ 1:
Question: How can I check my order status?
Answer: Customers can check their order status by providing their Order ID.

FAQ 2:
Question: What should I do if my product is damaged?
Answer: Customers should report the damaged product to customer support. A support ticket can be created for further assistance.

FAQ 3:
Question: How can I request a refund?
Answer: Customers can request a refund by providing their Order ID. Refund eligibility must be checked according to the refund policy.

Refund Policy:
1. Under this demo policy, an order must be Delivered to be eligible for a refund eligibility review.
2. Orders that are still Processing are not eligible for a refund under this demo policy.
3. Refund eligibility does not mean that a refund has been approved or processed.
4. Customers should contact support if they need further assistance.

Delivery Policy:
1. The expected delivery date is available in the order information.
2. If an order is delayed, customers can contact support for assistance.
